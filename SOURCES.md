## Historical-site rule

A historically important location is **not automatically a Sunnah visit** and does not automatically have a special reward. Cards therefore separate:

- established rite / Sunnah;
- historical significance;
- no special visit virtue claimed.

The “Prophet’s birthplace” card is deliberately phrased as a **traditionally associated** location. The project does not present the exact identification as certain and does not prescribe a devotional visit.

## Emergency numbers

The UI includes the numbers listed in current Saudi Hajj/Umrah guidance: 911, 999, 997, 998, 996, 937, and 1966, plus the two religious-guidance lines 8001222100 and 8001222400 listed in the official Umrah & Visit guide. Re-check the Ministry guide before each Hajj season.

The “Today” view is a planning aid, not a live operational timetable. It uses the browser’s Umm al-Qura calendar when available and includes a manual day selector. Actual lunar-date announcements, movement slots, transport, gate access and crowd-control directions must be confirmed from current authorities and the pilgrim’s licensed organizer.

The wellbeing timer is intentionally phrased as a general check rather than a fixed water-dose instruction because individual medical requirements can differ.
