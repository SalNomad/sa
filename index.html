If you are lost or separated from your group
  1. Move out of the walking flow to a safe, visible area.
  2. Use an official information/security point rather than following strangers.
  3. Call your group leader or 1966 for Hajj/Umrah support; use 911 for an emergency.
  4. Share your hotel name, gate number, or coordinates if you know them.

EVIDENCE & FRESHNESS

Sources and editorial rules

Operational information changes. Ritual guidance is linked to official Saudi material and primary hadith references where practical.

Editorial rule for historical sites

A place may be historically important without being a prescribed religious visit. This guide never turns historical association into a promise of spiritual reward unless a reliable text establishes it.
